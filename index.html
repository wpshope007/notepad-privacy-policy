Privacy Policy — Notes – Lezoker

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Notes – Lezoker ("the app") is a note-taking app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

No data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your information. It has no internet permission, contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and does not require an account to use.

Where your notes live

Everything you create — note titles, content, images, and folders — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Locked notes

Notes you move to the Locked folder are encrypted on-device using a key held in your device's secure hardware (Android Keystore), unlocked by your device's own fingerprint, face, or PIN. The developer has no access to this key or to locked note content.

Device backup

Like most Android apps, this app's local data may be included in Android's built-in device backup if you have that enabled in your phone's system settings. That backup is managed entirely by Android and your own Google account — the developer does not have access to it.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions beyond what's needed to attach images you choose to add to a note. It cannot access the internet, contacts, location, or any other device data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
